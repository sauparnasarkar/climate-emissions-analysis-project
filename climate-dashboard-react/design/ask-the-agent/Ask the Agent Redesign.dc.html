  1. Title covers emissions and climate outcomes. The subtitle says what the agent answers from.
  2. The input is its own field with a 40px send button. The focus ring goes around the whole field, not inside it. Prompts sit below the field, outside it.
  3. Six prompts in three columns: Historical trends, Climate outcomes (the two new prompts), Forecasts. Copy fixed on the India and 2040 prompts. The Tweaks panel switches to the 2×3 grid Claude Code is building.
  4. Answers: the question as a heading, a lead with figures, KPIs for the latest year, then charts. One highlighted series and greyed others replace the 10-colour spaghetti chart; the duplicate China chart is gone; bars use one colour.
  5. Every chart has a source and scope line. Each answer links to the dashboard page with the same data. The input becomes a sticky bar at the bottom with follow-up prompts above it.
AREA 2 RULES FOR AGENT ANSWERS
  1. Climate answers reuse the Correlation module’s figures and caveats: 0.520 slope with its CI, IPCC 0.45 (0.27–0.63) for comparison only, the illustrative chip, the Berkeley Earth vintage caveat.
  2. Pathway temperatures come from the module’s #scenarios translation (1.39 °C anchor, 0.520 slope, 40-country coverage). The agent doesn’t run its own calculation.
  3. No warming is attributed to any country. Country answers can point to global climate context, never to a country’s share of warming.
  4. The agent doesn’t correlate annual emissions with annual temperature.

CLIMATE OUTCOMES

How do temperature outcomes vary based on different emissions pathways?

ILLUSTRATIVE · IMPLIED OUTCOMES, NOT CLIMATE-MODEL PROJECTIONS

By 2040 the platform’s three emissions pathways imply 1.68–1.79 °C of warming above 1850–1900. Annual emissions in 2040 differ 2.2× between them, but the implied temperatures differ by only 0.11 °C, because 78% of the 2040 level (1.39 °C) was already reached by 2024. The pathways change only the warming still to come: the Aggressive pathway adds 28% less of it than BAU.

BAU · 20401.79 °C46,647 MtCO₂ a year
Moderate · 20401.73 °C30,400 MtCO₂ a year
Aggressive · 20401.68 °C21,611 MtCO₂ a year

Implied temperature by pathway, 2015–2040

°C above 1850–1900 · from cumulative CO₂ × the long-run slope
Starts from OWID World cumulative CO₂ to 2024. Pathways cover 40 countries; the rest of the world is held at its 2024 share.

How this is calculated

Slope used0.520 °C per 1,000 GtCO₂ (95% CI 0.480–0.559), 1850–2024, total CO₂ including land useIPCC for comparisonAR6 best estimate 0.45 (likely range 0.27–0.63)GasesCO₂ only, not an all-gas slope
A relationship in observed data, not a climate model. Results depend on the regression period, emissions source and assumptions. Based on Berkeley Earth file vintage 2025-01-10; a possible ~0.1 °C discrepancy with Berkeley Earth’s most recent published report text has not yet been reconciled.
